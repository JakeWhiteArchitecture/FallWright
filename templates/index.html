2 · Region

Click the top face of the roof structure: the top of the joists, where the firrings sit. Click more faces on the same level to merge them in; a click on another level starts a second roof, built separately. Openings voided in the slab become holes; walls on the deck and anything crossing it are cut out.

Picking generates nothing on its own. Press Enter or Build at the top right of the view to place the outlets and choose the buildup. After that the whole panel edits live. Stuck? Run fallwright() in the browser console.

Edges

Detected from the walls: a wall rising 1500 mm or more above the structure is an abutment, a lower one a parapet, no wall a free edge. Change any edge here; set free edges to gutter where an eaves gutter takes the water.

Outlets

Offsets run along the edge from the corner the outlet was placed from. A hopper goes through a wall (abutment or parapet) and always has a sump. In plan view, drag a sump or its ends and front edge, or an outlet within its sump.

Falls

The sump is the datum: its rim is the drain line the falls run up from. The main roof firrings are never under the minimum and thicken only where a sump needs the rim higher; otherwise the insulation sets the step into the sump.

Buildup

The insulation thickness comes from a U-value calculation done elsewhere. Every layer above the firrings is constant thickness, so the finished roof has the same falls.

Checks

Fallwright does not calculate U-values or condensation risk (Parts L and C), does not check roof covering fire performance (Part B), and does not design the deck, loads or wind uplift fixing. The falls scheme is design intent; the firrings are cut on site to suit the joists from the depths given. Schedules are setting-out information, not a quantity take-off. Verify all outputs before use.
